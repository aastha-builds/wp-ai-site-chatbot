How it works

1. Indexing admin button or on save

  1. Pages, FAQs, PDFspublished content and media library PDFs
  2. Chunkabout 500 tokens each, with overlap
  3. EmbedOpenAI embeddings, or the saved demo file
  4. Storewp_sitesage_chunks with title and URL

2. Answering POST /wp-json/sitesage/v1/ask

  1. Visitor questionnonce, rate limit per IP, length limit
  2. Retrievecosine similarity in PHP, top 4 chunks
  3. Ask the AIOpenAI or Claude get only the question and those chunks
  4. Answer + sourcesunder 120 words, linked page, or "I don't know"

3. Follow-up

  1. Lead formafter 2 questions or words like book, appointment, price
  2. Leads screenwp_sitesage_leads, CSV export
  3. Google Sheetswebhook to an Apps Script
  4. Talk to a personWhatsApp or email button

What it does

Learns your site

One click indexes pages, a FAQ post type and uploaded PDFs. Edited pages are re-indexed when you save them.

Answers with sources

Every answer comes only from your content, stays under 120 words and links the page it used.

Knows when to stop

When your content has no answer it says "I don't know that one, let me connect you with our team".

Captures leads

Asks for name, phone and email at the right moment, saves them in WordPress and posts them to Google Sheets.

Hands off to people

A "Talk to a person" button opens WhatsApp or email, whichever you set.

Safe by default

Nonces, capability checks, rate limits, a message length cap, and only the question plus retrieved text go to the AI.

Your choice of AI

OpenAI or Anthropic Claude, any model name. API keys are never shown back in full.

Measured, not guessed

wp sitesage eval runs 30 real patient questions and writes a pass or fail report.
