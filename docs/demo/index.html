Teeth Whitening

A brighter smile, safely

Coffee, tea, wine and time can leave teeth looking yellow or stained. Professional teeth whitening at BrightSmile is safer and much stronger than store-bought strips, because a dentist checks your teeth first and protects your gums.

Two ways to whiten

In-office whitening

One 90-minute visit. We protect your gums, apply a professional whitening gel and activate it in three short rounds. Most patients see their teeth get up to 8 shades whiter in a single appointment.

Take-home whitening kit

We make custom trays from a quick scan of your teeth. You wear them with whitening gel for 30 to 60 minutes a day for about two weeks.

Is whitening right for me?

Whitening works on natural teeth only. Crowns, veneers and fillings do not change color. We do not recommend whitening during pregnancy or for children under 16. Some people feel mild tooth sensitivity for a day or two, which our desensitizing gel helps.

How long do results last?

Results usually last one to three years. Touch-up gel refills keep your smile bright between treatments.

Implants

Replace missing teeth for good

A dental implant is a small titanium post that replaces the root of a missing tooth. Once it bonds with the jawbone, it holds a natural-looking crown that you brush and floss like your own teeth.

The implant process

  • Free implant consultation with a 3D cone beam scan to check your bone
  • Implant placement, a short procedure done with local anesthetic
  • Healing for 3 to 6 months while the implant fuses with the bone
  • Your custom crown is attached and your new tooth is complete

Am I a candidate?

Most healthy adults with a missing tooth can get an implant. If the jawbone is too thin, a bone graft can rebuild it first. Smoking and uncontrolled diabetes lower the success rate, so we discuss your health history at the consultation.

Success rate and comfort

Implants placed by Dr. Okafor have a success rate above 95 percent. Most patients say placement feels easier than a tooth extraction, and sedation is available if you feel nervous.

Implant-supported dentures

Missing many teeth? Four to six implants can hold a full arch of teeth securely, with no loose dentures or adhesive.

Insurance

Using your dental insurance

We are in-network with most PPO dental insurance plans and we file claims for you. Before your first visit, our team verifies your benefits so you know what your plan covers.

Plans we work with

  • Most employer and individual PPO plans: in-network
  • Out-of-network PPO plans: we still file the claim and you pay only your share
  • HMO and DHMO plans: not accepted
  • Medicaid: not accepted for adults; children's coverage is checked case by case

What insurance usually covers

Most plans cover two cleanings and checkups a year at 100 percent, fillings at about 80 percent and crowns or implants at about 50 percent, up to a yearly maximum. Cosmetic treatments such as teeth whitening are usually not covered.

Frequently asked questions

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes! We welcome new patients of all ages and usually have openings within the same week. You can call, message us on WhatsApp or ask the chat assistant to request an appointment.

Do you see children?

Yes. We see kids from age 2. We recommend a first visit by age 2 or 3 so your child gets comfortable with the dentist. Parents are welcome to stay in the room.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us right away at (415) 555-0142. We keep time free every day for same-day emergency visits. Outside office hours, call our emergency line at (415) 555-0199. If a tooth is knocked out, keep it moist in milk and come in within an hour.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. For treatment over $500 we offer in-house payment plans with 0% interest over 6 or 12 months, with no credit check.

How often should I get a dental cleaning?

Most people should have a checkup and professional cleaning every six months. Patients with gum disease may need a deep cleaning visit every three or four months.

I get nervous at the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. Tell us how you feel and we will go at your pace. We offer laughing gas (nitrous oxide) and mild oral sedation, noise-cancelling headphones and breaks whenever you need them.

Does whitening work on crowns or veneers?

No. Whitening gel only lightens natural tooth enamel. Crowns, veneers and fillings keep their color, so we may suggest whitening first and matching new crowns or veneers to your brighter shade.

How long is the first appointment?

Plan for about 90 minutes. That covers your medical history, digital x-rays, a full exam, a cleaning and time to talk through any treatment you may need.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give us at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations and missed appointments may have a $50 fee.

Are you open on Saturdays?

Yes, we are open on Saturday from 9 am to 1 pm. Saturday slots fill quickly, so book a week or two ahead. We are closed on Sundays.

Do you offer clear aligners instead of braces?

Yes. We offer clear aligners for teens and adults. Treatment usually takes 6 to 18 months. The consultation with a 3D scan is free.

What if I don't have dental insurance?

You can join the BrightSmile membership plan for $29 a month (adults) or $19 a month (children). It includes two cleanings and checkups a year, x-rays and 15% off treatment.

Are dental x-rays safe?

Yes. We use digital x-rays, which use up to 80% less radiation than old film x-rays. We only take x-rays when they are needed and we use a protective collar.

Can I have a root canal and a crown on the same day?

Often, yes. Dr. Okafor performs root canals in our clinic, and our in-house milling unit can make a same-day crown, so many patients finish both in one longer visit.

Do you treat bad breath or bleeding gums?

Yes. Bleeding gums and bad breath are often signs of gum disease. We check your gums at every visit and offer deep cleanings (scaling and root planing) to treat it.
